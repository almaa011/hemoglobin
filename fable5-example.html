KEY FINDING

This section is the whole page in miniature. A reader should be able to stop here and already have the answer. State the conclusion first, in plain language, with the single most important number bolded. Everything after this box is supporting detail — the "why" and the "how deep."

01 Interface Overview

Signal chain at a glance

The widget bus runs at 10 MHz with a 3.3V logic level. Keep paragraphs to 2–3 sentences — if a fourth sentence is needed, split it or convert to bullets instead.

03 Caveats and Conflicts

Flagging uncertainty and disagreement

Not every finding is clean — say so explicitly. Use these boxes rather than burying a caveat in a sentence.

WARNING

Use this box for a hard limit or failure mode a reader must not miss — e.g. exceeding max cable length causes intermittent bit errors above 8 MHz.

CONFLICT

Use this box when two sources disagree and you are not silently picking a winner — e.g. Source A lists max cable length as 2 m, Source B as 3 m under different shielding assumptions. Surface it, don't resolve it silently.

A pull-quote aside is for a short verbatim excerpt worth setting apart from the surrounding prose — used sparingly.
